Where to Stay

We have identified for you some accommodation facilities where you can stay during the days of the event and, why not, during the following days, taking advantage of the opportunity to explore the wonderful Amalfi Coast. We encourage you not to delay in booking: on those days the accommodation facilities in Vietri are generally "sold out"!

  • Hotel La Lucertola

    Via Cristoforo Colombo, 84019 Vietri sul Mare, Salerno

    Located on the cliffs of Vietri sul Mare a few steps from Torre Crestarella, La Lucertola offers modern rooms with a balcony overlooking the Gulf of Salerno. The property is completely renovated, and has a private beach with bar and solarium at an additional cost. All bright and air conditioned, rooms at Hotel La Lucertola offer free Wi-Fi, a minibar and a TV with satellite and pay-per-view channels. It offers single, double, triple and quadruple rooms. For information and availability, contact Serena at 089210255, stating that you are part of Nicoletta and Leto's wedding.

  • B&B Palazzo Della Guardia

    Via Osvaldo Costabile, 84019 Vietri sul Mare, Salerno

    The B&B is located in a recently renovated historic building in the center of the town, a few steps from the church of San Giovanni and about 10 minutes on foot from Torre Crestarella. The property has 5 double bedrooms. To ask for availability and a quote, call Mrs. Tiziana (3347645452) stating that you are part of Nicoletta and Leto's wedding.

  • B&B Calamarina

    Via Osvaldo Costabile, 84019 Vietri sul Mare, Salerno

    The B&B is located in the center of the town, 4 minutes from the church of San Giovanni and about 10 minutes on foot from Torre Crestarella. The property has 3 double bedrooms. To ask for availability and a quote, call Mrs. Amalia (3458814253) and let them know that you are part of Nicoletta and Leto's wedding.

  • B&B amaMi

    Via Roma, 84019 Vietri sul Mare, Salerno

    Located 5 minutes from the square of Vietri and next to the famous SOLIMENE ceramics, B&B amaMi has 5 double rooms, a shared terrace and wifi connection. For information on availability and prices contact Daniela directly (3934721918) and let them know that you are part of Nicoletta and Leto's wedding.
