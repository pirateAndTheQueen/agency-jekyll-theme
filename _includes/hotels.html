Where to Stay

We have identified for you some accommodation facilities where you can stay during the days of the event and, why not, during the following days, taking advantage of the opportunity to explore the wonderful Amalfi Coast. We encourage you not to delay in booking: on those days the accommodation facilities in Vietri are generally "sold out"!

  • Hotel La Lucertola

    Via Cristoforo Colombo, 29 84019 Vietri sul Mare, Salerno

    Located on the cliffs of Vietri sul Mare a few steps from Torre Crestarella, La Lucertola offers modern rooms with a balcony overlooking the Gulf of Salerno. The property is completely renovated, and has a private beach with bar and solarium at an additional cost. All bright and air conditioned, rooms at Hotel La Lucertola offer free Wi-Fi, a minibar and a TV with satellite and pay-per-view channels. It offers single, double, triple and quadruple rooms. For information and availability, contact Serena at 089210255, stating that you are part of Nicoletta and Leto's wedding.
