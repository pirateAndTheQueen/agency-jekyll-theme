Where to Stay

We have identified for you some accommodation facilities where you can stay during the days of the event and, why not, during the following days, taking advantage of the opportunity to explore the wonderful Amalfi Coast.

  • Lloyd's Baia Hotel

    Via Benedetto Croce, snc 84019 Vietri sul Mare, Salerno

    The Hotel is located at the beginning of the state road towards Salerno, a few steps from the centre of Vietri sul Mare, 1km from Torre Crestarella.

  • Hotel La Lucertola

    Via Cristoforo Colombo, 29 84019 Vietri sul Mare, Salerno

    The Hotel is located a few steps from Torre Crestarella.

  • B&B Umberto

    84019 Vietri sul Mare, Salerno

    The B&B is located in the center of the town, about 10 minutes walk from Torre Crestarella.

  • B&B La Casa del Bellino

    84019 Vietri sul Mare, Salerno

    The B&B is located in the center of the town (in the same building as the B&B Umberto), about 10 minutes walk from Torre Crestarella.

  • B&B Lumasa

    84019 Vietri sul Mare, Salerno

    The B&B is located in the center of the town, about 10 minutes walk from Torre Crestarella.
